Name: Recreation Center

Summary: Pools and gyms put moisture and people loads into one building.

Why a DMR package:
# One line per advantage, written as  - Short heading: one sentence
- Dry pool hall: Desiccant dehumidification protects the structure from condensation and corrosion
- Lower energy bills: Heat is recovered from the warm, humid pool exhaust
- Comfort at the doors: Cabinet and unit heaters stop drafts at busy entrances

Challenges:
- Pool humidity and condensation
- Large swings in occupancy
- Odor and air quality
