Name: Recreation Center

Summary: Pools, ice rinks and gyms put warm humid air, sub-freezing ice sheets and high spectator loads under one roof.

Why a DMR package:
# One line per advantage, written as  - Short heading: one sentence
- Dry pool hall: Desiccant dehumidification and corrosion-protected energy recovery protect the natatorium from condensation and chloramines
- Fog-free ice rink: Low-dew-point desiccant dehumidifiers keep moisture from forming fog over the ice or dripping from the roof structure
- Lower energy bills: Heat is recovered from the warm, humid pool exhaust
- Comfort in the stands and at the doors: Radiant, trench and cabinet heaters warm spectators and entrances without softening the ice

Challenges:
- Pool humidity and chloramine corrosion
- Low dew point required over the ice sheet to prevent fog and ceiling drip
- Large swings in spectator and athlete occupancy
- Odor and indoor air quality in locker and fitness areas
