Name: Convention Center

Summary: Exhibit halls go from empty to thousands of people in an hour.

Why a DMR package:
# One line per advantage, written as  - Short heading: one sentence
- Sized for the crowd, efficient when empty: Fan arrays turn down to a fraction of full airflow
- Outdoor air without the penalty: Energy recovery offsets the ventilation a full hall needs
- Fast replacement: Custom rooftop units match existing openings

Challenges:
- Huge swings in occupancy
- Long air throws in high spaces
- Large outdoor air volumes
