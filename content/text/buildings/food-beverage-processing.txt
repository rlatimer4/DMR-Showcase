Name: Food & Beverage Processing

Summary: Processing rooms are washed down daily and must stay dry and clean.

Why a DMR package:
# One line per advantage, written as  - Short heading: one sentence
- Dry after wash-down: Desiccant drying gets rooms back in production sooner
- Sanitary construction: Stainless interiors, sloped drain pans and UV-C
- No fog at the doors: Dry air stops condensation and frost at cold-room doors

Challenges:
- Wash-down humidity
- Sanitation
- Temperature-controlled process rooms
