Name: Multifamily Residential

Summary: Apartments need individual control and individual billing in every unit.

Why a DMR package:
# One line per advantage, written as  - Short heading: one sentence
- Every resident in control: Each apartment has its own system and its own bill
- Fresh corridors: Energy recovery ventilation without the heating penalty
- Small footprint: Equipment that fits closets, ceilings and balconies

Challenges:
- Individual control in each unit
- Ventilation for corridors
- Limited space for equipment
