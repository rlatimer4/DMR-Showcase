Name: Industrial Manufacturing

Summary: Heavy manufacturing exhausts large air volumes and generates its own heat.

Why a DMR package:
# One line per advantage, written as  - Short heading: one sentence
- Pressurized plant: Make-up air replaces large process exhaust volumes
- Even heat end to end: Air turnover covers a long production floor from a few units
- Process heat put to work: Plate exchangers recover energy from exhaust

Challenges:
- Large exhaust volumes
- Process heat
- Harsh environment for equipment
