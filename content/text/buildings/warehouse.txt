Name: Warehouse

Summary: Large-volume buildings lose heat through dock doors and stratify from floor to roof.

Why a DMR package:
# One line per advantage, written as  - Short heading: one sentence
- Even temperature floor to roof: Air rotation ends stratification in high bays
- Warm docks: Unit heaters offset the cold at open dock doors
- Low operating cost: Direct-fired heating puts nearly all of its fuel energy into the space

Challenges:
- Heat lost at dock doors
- Stratification in high bays
- Low operating cost
