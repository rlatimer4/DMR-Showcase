Name: Mid-Rise Office

Summary: Mid-rise offices balance first cost against energy use.

Why a DMR package:
# One line per advantage, written as  - Short heading: one sentence
- Energy code compliance: Outdoor air is pre-conditioned with recovered energy
- Zone-by-zone comfort: Every zone has its own heat pump or VAV box
- Small mechanical footprint: Floor-by-floor units need only a closet on each floor

Challenges:
- Energy code compliance
- Zone control on every floor
- Mechanical space on each floor
