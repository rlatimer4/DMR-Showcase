Name: Data Center

Summary: Data halls reject heat every hour of the year, so the equipment is chosen for efficiency and uptime.

Why a DMR package:
# One line per advantage, written as  - Short heading: one sentence
- Uptime: Fan arrays and N+1 plant keep cooling running through a failure or a service call
- Speed to build: Cooling plant and air handlers arrive factory assembled and tested
- Room to grow: Air cooling today, with a path to liquid cooling as rack densities rise

Challenges:
- Cooling that never stops, including during maintenance
- Rising rack densities
- Short construction schedules
