Name: High-Rise Office

Summary: Tall office buildings are leased by the floor and refit often.

Why a DMR package:
# One line per advantage, written as  - Short heading: one sentence
- Fits the building you have: Rooftop units are built to the existing curb, steel and crane limits
- Leasable ceiling height: Compact terminals and small ducts keep ceilings shallow
- Tenant flexibility: VRF and mini-splits add cooling for one tenant without touching the base building

Challenges:
- Limited ceiling and shaft space
- Tenant-by-tenant flexibility
- Replacing rooftop plant on an occupied building
