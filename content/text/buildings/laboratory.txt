Name: Research Laboratory

Summary: Research buildings hold wet labs, dry labs, vivariums and clean spaces side by side, each with its own ventilation, pressure and humidity needs.

Why a DMR package:
# One line per advantage, written as  - Short heading: one sentence
- Safe once-through air: Custom air handlers and fan arrays keep hoods and animal rooms supplied through a fan failure
- Exhaust energy recovered: Plate exchangers and wheels reclaim heat with no cross-contamination
- Each room held in range: Airflow tracking terminals, clean steam and desiccant drying hold pressure, temperature and humidity room by room
- Instrument heat handled at the zone: Sensible cooling terminals remove equipment heat without raising the air change rate

Challenges:
- 100% outdoor air and high air change rates
- Room pressure and containment
- Tight humidity for animal holding and instruments
- No downtime for hoods and animal rooms
