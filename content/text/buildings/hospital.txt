Name: Hospital

Summary: Hospitals run around the clock and cannot lose airflow, so the equipment is chosen for redundancy and infection control.

Why a DMR package:

- Humidity in range: Clean-steam humidifiers hold surgical suites within code limits
- Built for renovation: Air handlers ship in sections that fit existing mechanical rooms

Challenges:
- No downtime for air systems
- Infection control
- Humidity limits in surgical spaces
