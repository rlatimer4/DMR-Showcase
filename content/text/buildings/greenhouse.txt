Name: Greenhouse

Summary: Plants release moisture all day, and the glass loses heat all night.

Why a DMR package:
# One line per advantage, written as  - Short heading: one sentence
- Healthy crop: Humidity is held where mould and mildew do not take hold
- Even conditions: Heat and moisture are distributed across the whole growing floor
- Lower fuel use: Desiccant drying avoids venting heated air to the outdoors

Challenges:
- High humidity from plants
- Heat loss through glass
- Even conditions across the crop
