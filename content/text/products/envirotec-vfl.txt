Name: VFL Parallel Fan Powered Low-Height VAV Terminals

Summary: VFL parallel fan-powered low-height VAV terminal units handle 150 to 2,300 CFM primary air within an 11" to 12.5" casing profile for shallow floor-to-floor heights. Pairs an intermittent ECM heating fan with hot water coil or electric reheat options under AHRI 880 certification.

Key points:
- Low-profile 11" to 12.5" cabinet fits shallow floor-to-floor ceiling plenums
- 150 to 2,300 CFM primary airflow with intermittent ECM parallel heating fan
- Optional hot water coil or electric heat for perimeter zone heating
- AHRI 880 certified performance and UL 1995 listed
