Name: VL Vertical Low-Profile Fan Coil Units

Summary: Vertical low-profile floor-mounted fan coil units delivering 200 to 600 CFM beneath low window sills and glazed curtain-wall perimeters in commercial and institutional buildings.

Key points:
- Low-sill 14" to 16" vertical perimeter cabinet fits cleanly under full-height glazing
- 200 to 600 CFM airflow capacity with 2-pipe or 4-pipe hydronic coils and optional top or angled grilles
- Sloped drain pan and removable access panels simplify filter changes and periodic cleaning
- AHRI 440 certified performance and UL 1995 safety listing for perimeter office and classroom zones
