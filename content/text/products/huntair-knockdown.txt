Name: Knockdown Air Handlers

Summary: Huntair knockdown air handlers bring 2,000 to 120,000+ CFM custom construction into tight sites on welded structural tube bases via two platforms: Smooth-Exterior 2"/4" structural panelized units shipped disassembled on pallets (field or palletized knockdown), or Huntair FPC 2-5/8" injected-foam aluminum-framed panels fastened by gullwing clips for simple field or factory assembly.

Key points:
- Smooth-Exterior field or palletized knockdown: components ship disassembled on pallets
- Smooth panels (4" ±12" WC L/240; 2" ±8" WC L/200), modified thermal break, 2" upturned floor lip
- Huntair FPC field/factory knockdown: 2-5/8" injected foam, epoxy thermal break, gullwing clips (L/500 @ ±10" WC)
- Full powder coat w/ antimicrobial & FDA options, washdown, OSHPD, BABA & FANWALL arrays
