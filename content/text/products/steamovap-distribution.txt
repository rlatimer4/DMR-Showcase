Name: Steam Distribution

Summary: Steam distribution equipment: duct and air handler manifolds, and space blowers that distribute steam directly into a room where there is no duct.

Key points:
- Single stainless dispersion tubes, SDU short-absorption manifolds, and direct-room fan packs
- Multi-tube short-absorption manifolds achieve complete absorption down to 6"–12" in AHUs
- Corrosion-resistant 304L or 316L stainless steel manifolds with condensate drain header
