Name: Air Handlers with Low-Temp Desiccant Dehumidification

Summary: Custom air handlers from the Nortek Air Solutions brands (Temtrol, Governair, Huntair, Mammoth, Venmar, and Ventrol) with low-temperature desiccant dehumidification. Built on welded structural tube bases with polyurethane spray/injected foam casings (3"/3.5" ITF or 2"/4" smooth-exterior panelized or 2-5/8" Huntair FPC) and perforated acoustic liners (all except Temtrol LC & Huntair FPC).

Key points:
- Low-temperature regenerated desiccant delivers dry air for sensible VAV & chilled beams
- Welded structural tube base with polyurethane spray or injected foam insulation
- Available in brand-specific 3"/3.5" ITF (±10" WC L/240) or 2"/4" smooth-exterior / 2-5/8" FPC casings
- Perforated acoustic liners available on all custom AHU platforms (except Temtrol LC & Huntair FPC)

Specifications:
- Supply dewpoint: Low-humidity control down to −40°F to +45°F dewpoint (1,000–80,000+ CFM)

- Components: Pre-cooling CHW/DX coil, silica-gel desiccant rotor, reactivation heater, FANWALL array
- Applications: Operating rooms, cleanrooms, pharma, battery dry rooms and ice arenas
