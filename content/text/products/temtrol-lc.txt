Name: Temtrol LC Air Handler

Summary: Temtrol LC is Nortek's lowest-cost semi-custom indoor/outdoor AHU, delivering up to 20,000 CFM in 2" injected-foam panelized construction (R-16 walls and base) on a bolted formed galvanized steel base rated to <1% leakage and L/200 deflection at ±8" WC. Sized around 96"W x 72"H x 48" modules variable in 1" increments with galvanized, stainless, or aluminum skins.

Key points:
- Semi-custom indoor/outdoor AHU up to 20,000 CFM with <1% casing leakage and L/200 deflection at ±8" WC
- 2" injected-foam panelized construction (R-16 base & walls) on a bolted formed galvanized steel base in GS, SS, or AL
- Modular 96"W x 72"H x 48" footprints variable in 1" increments across horizontal, side-by-side heat wheel ER, and vertical layouts
- Direct-drive plenum PF09 or FANWALL EC fans plus economizer with RA and EA fans (module delivery only; no knockdown or perf liners)
