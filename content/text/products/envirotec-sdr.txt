Name: SDR Single Duct VAV Terminals

Summary: Single-duct VAV terminal units covering 100 to 8,000 CFM across standard inlet sizes, equipped with Enviro-Tec's FlowStar™ multi-point airflow sensor for accurate zone volume modulation.

Key points:
- Wide 100 to 8,000 CFM single-duct VAV capacity range across round and rectangular inlets
- Integral FlowStar™ center-averaging airflow probe delivers accurate velocity sensing (±5%) with low pressure drop
- Available with 1- to 4-row hot water reheat coils or staged / SCR proportional electric heat
- AHRI 880 certified and UL 1995 listed with fiberglass-free IAQ liner options for healthcare and schools
