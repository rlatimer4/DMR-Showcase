Name: Katherm QK nano Trench Heating

Summary: Katherm QK nano is an ultra-shallow 70 mm (2.75") high × 165 mm wide fan-assisted hydronic floor trench convector engineered for post-tensioned slabs, raised access floors, and thin floor toppings. Pairs a miniaturized EC tangential fan with a copper/brass finned convector for fast perimeter heat.

Key points:
- Ultra-shallow 70 mm (2.75") × 165 mm profile fits post-tensioned slabs and raised floors
- Miniaturized EC tangential fan boosts convective output along tall glass walls
- Durable copper/brass finned hydronic convector in a compact architectural trench
