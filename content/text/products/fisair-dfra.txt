Name: DFRA Dehumidifiers

Summary: Versatile industrial desiccant rotor dehumidifiers delivering 450 to 6,000 m³/h (~265 to 3,530 CFM) of dry air using a bacteriostatic, washable silica-gel rotor. Backed by Fisair's 55+ years of humidity engineering, Fisair Selection Tool (FST) software, competitive lead times, and a 2-year standard warranty.

Key points:
- Drying capacities from 4 to 42 kg/h (8.8 to 92.6 lb/h) across ~265 to 3,530 CFM process airflow
- Bacteriostatic washable silica-gel desiccant rotor tested against bacterial and fungal growth
- Multiple reactivation options (electric, steam, gas, or hot water) match available plant utilities
- Selected via FST (Fisair Selection Tool) with factory application engineering support and a 2-year standard warranty
