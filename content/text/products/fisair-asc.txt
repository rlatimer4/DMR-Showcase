Name: Diphusair ASC Humidifiers

Summary: Self-generating Diphusair ASC steam humidifiers use pressurized superheated boiler water in a cleanable heat exchanger to generate chemical-free clean steam at 55 to 1,400 lb/h without electric heaters or boiler carry-over chemicals.

Key points:
- Uses pressurized superheated boiler water (>105°C to 180°C) as the clean thermal energy source
- Separates closed-loop heating water from potable, softened, or DI water for chemical-free humidification
- High steam capacity from 25 to 635 kg/h (55 to 1,400 lb/h) suited for hospitals, labs, and cleanrooms
- 316L stainless steel evaporating chamber and removable scale-collector pan simplify maintenance
