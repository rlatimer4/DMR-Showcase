Name: Valve Piping Packages

Summary: Factory-assembled and tested 1/2", 3/4", and 1" sweat or union-end brazed hydronic valve piping packages engineered for fan coil and blower coil units. Configurable with 2-way or 3-way PICV or characterized ball valves, isolation valves, Y-strainers, P/T ports, and autoflow balancing.

Key points:
- Factory-brazed and pressure-tested 1/2", 3/4", and 1" sweat or union-end packages
- 2-way or 3-way modulating and two-position PICV or characterized ball valves
- Includes ball isolation valves, Y-strainer with blowdown, P/T ports, and autoflow
