Name: Venkon Fan Coils

Summary: Kampmann Venkon VDI 6022 hygienically certified EC fan coils deliver quiet 2-pipe or 4-pipe comfort down to 19 dBA (NC-15), while the new Venkon XXL expands the platform to 4 ducted recirculation sizes handling extra-high static pressures up to 4" H2O. Double-walled Sendzimir galvanized cabinets feature 1" rock wool insulation, 4" MERV 8 or MERV 13 filters, and removable insulated stainless steel condensate pans.

Key points:
- Venkon VDI 6022 hygienic EC fan coils operate down to 19 dBA (NC-15); Venkon XXL handles up to 4" H2O external static across 4 sizes
- 0–10 V stepless EC radial fan (120 V or 208–277 V 1-phase) with factory electromechanical terminal block and safety switch
- 2-pipe or 4-pipe copper/aluminum heat exchanger (300 psi max, 41–194°F EWT, 20–50% glycol) with bottom-access 4" MERV 8 or MERV 13 filter
- Double-walled Sendzimir galvanized casing with 1" rock wool plus removable 1/4" (6 mm) closed-cell insulated stainless condensate pan (3/4" drain + overflow)
