Name: Controls and Thermostats

Summary: Solid-state SCR/SSR power controllers, step controllers, thermostats and UL 508A control panels built for precise modulation of electric heating loads.

Key points:
- Zero-cross SSR and SCR proportional controllers smoothly match electric heat to partial loads
- Line-voltage, low-voltage (24V) and digital programmable wall thermostats
- Accepts 0–10 VDC, 4–20 mA, PWM and BMS integration signals
- Available in NEMA 1, NEMA 4 and NEMA 4X control enclosures
