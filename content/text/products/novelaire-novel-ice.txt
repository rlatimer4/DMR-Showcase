Name: Novel-Ice Ice Arena Dehumidifiers

Summary: NovelAire PrecisionDRY-Ice+ (DESDX-CR) systems combine DX refrigeration with a desiccant rotor in a 600–15,000 CFM Cromer-cycle cabinet purpose-built for year-round indoor ice arenas, hockey rinks, and curling clubs. Using 90–105°F condenser-reclaim regeneration without a separate regen airstream or gas burner, units handle up to 100% OA at up to 40% lower operating cost than gas-fired units.

Key points:
- PrecisionDRY-Ice+ (DESDX-CR) holds rink dew points (28–38°F, e.g., ~36°F DP skating/NHL; ~20°F DP curling) to stop fog, drip, and soft ice
- Cromer-cycle DX + desiccant slipstream uses 90–105°F reclaimed condenser heat with no separate regen airstream and up to 40% lower OPEX vs. gas
- 600 to 15,000 CFM 2" R-13 foam double-wall cabinets (frequently paired, e.g., dual units at Kirkland Ice Plex delivering 478 lb/hr each at 50.5°F / 19.2 gr/lb)
- ECM/VFD fans, speed-controlled wheels, standalone HMI or BACnet BMS controls, sized to the ASHRAE 0.4% dehumidification design day
