Name: Novel-Ice Ice Arena Dehumidifiers

Summary: Hybrid DX refrigeration and desiccant dehumidification units built for indoor ice arenas, using reclaimed compressor condenser heat to regenerate the desiccant wheel without natural gas burners.

Key points:
- Delivers low, below-freezing dew points to prevent rink fog, ceiling drip and soft ice
- Regenerates the desiccant wheel with waste condenser heat, eliminating gas combustion
- Minimizes sensible heat added to the arena, reducing load on the ice plant
