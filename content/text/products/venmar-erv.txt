Name: Energy Recovery and DOAS Units

Summary: Venmar CES Energy Recovery and 100% outdoor air DOAS systems span 1,000 to 60,000+ CFM using 3" double-wall ITF framed construction on welded structural tube base frames. Rated to ±10" WC at L/240 deflection with polyurethane spray foam or fiberglass insulation, no thru-metal except fasteners, perforated acoustic liners, packaged refrigeration, and a 2" recessed Polyurea washdown floor option.

Key points:
- 3" double-wall ITF frame on welded structural tube base (±10" WC, L/240 deflection)
- No thru-metal except fasteners; polyurethane spray foam or fiberglass + perforated liners
- 2" recessed floor with watertight insulating Polyurea membrane & washdown option
- AHRI 1060 wheels or counterflow/crossflow plates with packaged DX, ASHP, CHW, or gas heat
