Name: Coolant Distribution Units

Summary: ServerCool Coolant Distribution Units (CDUs) from Nortek Data Center Cooling deliver temperature-controlled liquid coolant directly to high-density racks in new and retrofit data centers. Available in 48U-footprint 300 kW to 1.2+ MW enclosures and multi-megawatt 1 MW to 5+ MW skidded systems.

Key points:
- 300 kW to 1.2+ MW enclosed 48U CDUs and 1 MW to 5+ MW skidded systems
- Built-in 2N redundancy with hot-swappable pumps and dual power feeds
- Compact footprint starting with standard 48U rack width

Specifications:
- Cooling capacity: 300 kW to 1.2+ MW (48U enclosed) and 1 MW to 5+ MW (skidded multi-MW)
- Redundancy: 2N dual VFD stainless steel pumps, dual power feeds, and 50 µm filtration
- Heat exchanger: 316L stainless steel brazed/gasketed plate exchanger (ASHRAE W1–W4 supply)
- Controls: Dewpoint-tracking PID valve control with BACnet IP, Redfish and Modbus TCP/IP
