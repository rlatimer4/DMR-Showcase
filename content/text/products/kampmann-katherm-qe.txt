Name: Katherm QE Electric Trench Heating

Summary: Katherm QE electric perimeter floor trenches (190 mm wide × 110 mm deep) combine a modulating stainless-steel electric heating element with a quiet tangential EC fan and thermal cutouts. Operates via 0–10V BAS or room thermostat without running hydronic piping to exterior glass walls.

Key points:
- 190 mm × 110 mm perimeter floor trench with no hydronic piping required
- Modulating stainless-steel electric heating element with thermal safety cutouts
- Tangential EC fan with 0–10V BAS or room thermostat control
