Name: DFRB Dehumidifiers

Summary: Compact AISI 304 stainless steel desiccant dehumidifiers delivering 140 to 700 m³/h (82 to 412 CFM) of dry process air with a self-regulating PTC electric heater and washable silica-gel rotor.

Key points:
- Drying capacities from 0.5 to 4.2 kg/h (1.1 to 9.3 lb/h) in a compact corrosion-resistant AISI 304 stainless cabinet
- Washable high-absorption silica-gel rotor tested against bacteria and fungi
- Self-regulating PTC reactivation heating element reduces standby energy draw and simplifies electrical hookup
- Direct-drive fans and ducted air spigots suit clean storage vaults, laboratories, and mechanical utility rooms
