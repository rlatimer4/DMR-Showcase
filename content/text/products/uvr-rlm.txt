Name: RLM Xtreme UV-C Fixtures

Summary: A modular UV-C fixture system for air handlers of virtually any size, offering very high output 33", 36", 42", and 61" lamps with up to six power supplies per NEMA-4 housing. Improves coil heat transfer and cuts manual cleaning across commercial and institutional AHUs.

Key points:
- Scales to air handlers of virtually any width and height
- Very high output 33", 36", 42", and 61" germicidal lamps
- Up to six 120V–277V power supplies per NEMA-4 housing with 12V safety interlock
- Improves coil heat transfer and reduces maintenance cleaning
