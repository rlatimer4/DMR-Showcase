Name: Hydronic Radiators

Summary: Welded flat-tube steel panel radiators and baseboards (UF-2, UF-3, UF-4 and high-output finned VLX series) for architectural commercial, healthcare and institutional perimeter heating.

Key points:
- Low-profile UF-2 (6"), UF-3 (9") and UF-4 (12") baseboards plus high-output finned VLX wall panels
- 2 mm cold-rolled welded steel waterways rated to 85 psig working (128 psig optional) pressure
- Performs well with low-temperature hydronic loops (down to 140°F) as well as standard high-temp systems
- Smooth wipe-clean surfaces for healthcare and labs, custom lengths and 100+ RAL powder-coat finishes
