Name: Sensors and Humidity Controllers

Summary: Room and duct humidity sensors and PID controllers with LCD setpoint adjustment, ±1.5% to ±2% RH sensing accuracy, and optional BACnet or Modbus networking. Pairs directly with SteamOVap humidifiers for tight, automated space and duct humidity control.

Key points:
- Duct and wall RH/temperature transmitters (±1.5% to ±2% RH)
- Standalone PID humidity controllers with 0–10V / 4–20mA signals
- Optional BACnet and Modbus building automation interfaces
