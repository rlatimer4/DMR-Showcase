Name: DFRC Dehumidifiers

Summary: Industrial and portable self-supporting desiccant dehumidifiers built on a protective tubular crash frame for rugged industrial drying, temporary construction dehumidification, and space humidity control.

Key points:
- Protective stainless steel tubular frame and carry-ready roof shielding resist site handling and weather exposure
- Available in industrial capacities from 1,200 to 15,000 m³/h (706 to 8,828 CFM) removing 9 to 106 kg/h at 20°C / 60% RH
- High-performance washable silica-gel rotor holds low relative humidity across cold or hot ambient air
- Plug-and-play electrical reactivation and PLC controls simplify rapid deployment and industrial integration
