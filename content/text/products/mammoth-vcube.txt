Name: V-Cube Vertical Self-Contained Systems

Summary: Vertical self-contained VAV systems in a compact footprint, from 9 to 150 tons and 4,000 to 40,000 CFM. Available air-cooled, water-cooled or glycol-cooled, as a water-source heat pump, and as a chilled-water air handler.
