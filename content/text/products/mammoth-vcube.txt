Name: V-Cube Vertical Self-Contained Systems

Summary: Mammoth V-Cube semi-custom vertical self-contained VAV systems span 9 to 180 tons (3,000 to 43,000 CFM) using 2"/3" double-wall ITF cabinets on welded structural tube bases (±10" WC @ L/240). Built with lead variable-speed compressors, CNC tube bending, shell-and-tube or brazed-plate heat exchangers, 36" factory-charged split modules with isolation valves, and optional FANWALL with Coplanar Silencers.

Key points:
- 9 to 180 tons (3,000–43,000 CFM) water/glycol/air-cooled DX, WSHP, or CHW VAV self-contained systems
- Lead variable-speed compressors, CNC tube bending for minimal pipe stress, and HGRH or drainable condenser-water reheat
- Splits into 36" modules factory-charged with isolation valves for narrow mechanical rooms and freight elevator retrofits
- Direct-drive FANWALL (optional Coplanar Silencer) or belt-drive fans, mixing box, waterside economizer, and integral electric heat

Specifications:
- Capacity & systems: 9 to 180 tons (3,000–43,000 CFM); water/glycol/air-cooled DX (shell & tube HX), WSHP (brazed plate HX), or CHW
- Casing & thermal: 2"/3" ITF double-wall on welded tube base (±10" WC @ L/240); no thru-metal except fasteners; optional 2" Polyurea floor
- Refrigeration & ingress: Lead VSC + CNC tube bending; splits into 36" modules factory-charged with isolation valves; HGRH or condenser-water reheat
- Airside & options: Direct-drive FANWALL (w/ Coplanar Silencer), WiSE/CW/HW/DX coils, integral electric heat, packaged DDC controls
