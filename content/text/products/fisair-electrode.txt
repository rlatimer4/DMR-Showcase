Name: Diphusair Electrode Humidifiers

Summary: Self-contained Diphusair electrode humidifiers generate 2 to 130 kg/h (4.4 to 286 lb/h) of clean steam per unit—or up to 780 kg/h in master/slave arrays—from potable water. Features disposable or cleanable cylinders and touchscreen controls with native Modbus and BACnet IP/MS/TP.

Key points:
- 2 to 130 kg/h (4.4 to 286 lb/h) per unit; master/slave arrays up to 780 kg/h
- Clean electrode steam generation on standard potable water (125–1,250 µS/cm)
- Choice of disposable or cleanable steam cylinders across 208V–575V power
- Touchscreen controller with native Modbus, BACnet IP, and BACnet MS/TP
