Name: Electric Duct Heaters

Summary: Custom-built electric duct heaters (slip-in, flanged, PD packaged, 7200 Plenum and HotPod supplemental in-line heaters) engineered in the USA for AHUs, terminal boxes and ductwork.

Key points:
- Open-coil 80/20 NiCr and finned tubular stainless elements with heavy-duty galvanized steel frames
- HotPod 6" and 8" ETL zero-clearance in-line heaters fix cold zones without central system upgrades
- Staged contactor, vernier and proportional SSR modulating controls with primary/secondary thermal cutouts
- UL 1995 / CSA listed across single- and three-phase commercial voltages up to 600V
