Name: DLN Xtreme UV-C Fixtures

Summary: A dual-lamp very high output NEMA-4 UV-C fixture that mounts on the exterior of a plenum or duct to deliver double irradiance inside the airstream. Built for high-velocity coils and demanding microbial control while allowing external ballast access.

Key points:
- Exterior duct and plenum mounting with NEMA-4 enclosure
- Dual-lamp configuration doubles UV-C dose density
- 16" to 61" very high output 254 nm lamps
- 120V–277V electronic power supply
