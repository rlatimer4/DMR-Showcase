Name: CFL Series Flow Fan Powered Low-Height VAV Terminals

Summary: Low-height series-flow fan-powered VAV terminal units in a shallow 11" to 12" cabinet spanning 100 to 4,100 CFM for congested ceiling plenums. Pairs a variable-speed ECM motor with the patented FlowStar™ multi-point primary inlet airflow sensor yielding 230% to 290% signal amplification for accurate low-flow turndown.

Key points:
- Shallow 11" to 12" cabinet profile fits above structural beams and crowded MEP plenums across 100 to 4,100 CFM
- FlowStar™ airflow sensor delivers 230–290% amplification (1.50" w.g. @ 3,000 FPM vs. 0.56" Pitot) for stable low-flow VAV control
- Hinged bottom and side access panels simplify CR-series ECM blower and electric/HW coil servicing without dropping ceiling grid
- AHRI 880 certified and UL 1995 listed with hot water or staged/SCR electric reheat options
