Name: VRF Ceiling Cassettes

Summary: GREE VRF ceiling cassette indoor units span 5,000 to 48,000 BTU/h across one-way, two-way, 360° four-way, and compact 2'×2' ceiling-grid models. Each cassette includes a built-in high-head condensate lift pump, 4-way independent louver control, electronic expansion valve, and quiet 26–32 dBA acoustics.

Key points:
- 5,000 to 48,000 BTU/h across compact 2'×2', standard 4-way, round-flow, and 1-way styles
- Built-in high-head condensate lift pump, EEV, and 4-way independent louver swing
- Quiet ceiling operation down to 26–32 dBA for offices and hospitality
