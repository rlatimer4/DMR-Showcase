Name: Light Duty Cleanroom Ceiling Grid

Summary: Huntair Light Duty cleanroom ceiling grids offer a lightweight gasketed aluminum T-bar solution for ISO Class 6–8 rooms such as USP 797/800 compounding pharmacies and tissue culture labs. Accepts standard 2'×4' and 2'×2' CleanPak FFUs, HEPA modules, blank panels, and LED troffers at lower installed weight and cost.

Key points:
- Light-duty gasketed T-bar cleanroom grid designed for ISO Class 6–8 environments
- Ideal for USP 797/800 compounding pharmacies, tissue culture, and clean assembly
- Accepts standard 2'×4' and 2'×2' CleanPak FFUs, HEPA modules, and LED troffers
