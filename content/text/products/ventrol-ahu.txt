Name: Custom Air Handlers

Summary: Ventrol smooth-exterior custom air handlers deliver 1,500 to 180,000+ CFM using 2" (R-16 foam / R-8.3 fiberglass; ±8" WC) or 4" (R-32 foam / R-16.7 fiberglass; ±12" WC @ L/240, up to 0.5% leakage) structural panelized construction on welded tube bases. Available with PolyBlock True No Thru Metal, all-aluminum or stainless construction, 2" upturned floor lip, washdown, standing seam roofs to 39'-8", and factory catwalks.

Key points:
- Smooth-exterior 4" (R-32 foam; ±12" WC @ L/240, to 0.5% leakage) or 2" (R-16 foam; ±8" WC) structural panels on welded tube base
- Modified thermal break or PolyBlock True No Thru Metal (1" PolyBlock strips eliminate fastener thermal bridging)
- All-aluminum or stainless options, third-layer standing seam roof up to 39'-8" span, and cantilevered/bolt-on catwalks
- Standard 2" upturned floor lip, washdown construction, stacked air tunnels, Gen IV FANWALL arrays, and field/palletized knockdown
