Name: Air Handlers with Gas-Phase Filtration

Summary: Custom air handlers from the Nortek Air Solutions brands (Temtrol, Governair, Huntair, Mammoth, Venmar, and Ventrol) integrating gas-phase molecular filtration sections for airports, museums, and healthcare. Built on welded structural tube bases with polyurethane spray/injected foam casings and perforated acoustic liners across Nortek's custom ITF, smooth-exterior panelized, or Huntair FPC platforms.

Key points:
- Removes jet exhaust, VOCs, ozone, and gaseous odors in custom Nortek air handlers
- Welded structural tube base with polyurethane spray/injected foam insulation
- Configured in 3"/3.5" ITF (±10" WC L/240), 2"/4" smooth panelized, or 2-5/8" Huntair FPC
- Perforated acoustic liners available (all except Temtrol LC & Huntair FPC)

Specifications:
- Airflow range: 1,500 to 100,000+ CFM custom AHU on welded structural tube base frame
