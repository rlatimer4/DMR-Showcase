Name: FANWALL Retrofit Systems

Summary: Governair Gen IV FANWALL retrofit systems upgrade aging belt-drive centrifugal fans with lower-vibration, lighter direct-drive plug fan arrays featuring 12" to 27" wheels, Zero-System-Effect backdraft dampers, Coplanar Silencers, and IE5+ (200,000+ hr L10) or ECMi motors with System Optimization Controls.

Key points:
- Gen IV FANWALL construction with reduced vibration and weight; AMCA 204-96 Class BV-5 Grade G.55 balance (0.022 in/s peak)
- 12" to 27" wheels (AMCA 211/311 certified to 22") with 1–15 HP (20/30 HP on 27"), IE5+ ferrite-magnet, or ECMi motors
- Components pass through 36" doors with Zero-System-Effect backdraft dampers and System Optimization Controls
- Optional Coplanar Silencers and FANWALL EC compact length-saving configuration (saves 12"–24" unit length)
