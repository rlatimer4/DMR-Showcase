Name: DFRD Dehumidifiers

Summary: Double-fan closed-circuit desiccant dehumidifiers delivering 300 to 700 m³/h (176 to 412 CFM) with completely isolated process and reactivation airstreams for demanding industrial and process drying.

Key points:
- Dual independent process and reactivation fans eliminate cross-air leakage in closed-loop drying systems
- Drying capacities from 3 to 4.2 kg/h (6.6 to 9.3 lb/h) at 20°C / 60% RH in a compact stainless enclosure
- Achieves low process dew points without mixing humid outdoor ambient air into the dried supply stream
- Suited to plastic resin hopper drying, seed storage, pharmaceutical packaging, and archival vaults
