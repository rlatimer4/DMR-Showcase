Name: ISE Steam Exchange Humidifier

Summary: Steam-to-steam exchange humidifiers delivering 4 to 600 lb/h (1.8 to 272 kg/h) of chemical-free clean steam heated indirectly by central boiler steam inside a 316L stainless heat exchanger.

Key points:
- Completely isolates chemically treated plant boiler steam from clean potable, softened, or RO humidification water
- Wide capacity range from 4 to 600 lb/h (1.8 to 272 kg/h) meets ASHRAE 170 healthcare and laboratory IAQ standards
- Cleanable 316L stainless steel heat exchanger coil and evaporation tank reduce long-term scaling maintenance
- Modulating control valve and touch-screen controller support native BACnet and Modbus BMS integration
