Name: HL Horizontal Low-Profile Fan Coil Units

Summary: Horizontal low-profile HL fan coil units (200 to 1,200 CFM) fit shallow 10" to 11" ceiling soffits and guestroom entries. Features quiet ECM motors, 2-pipe or 4-pipe hydronic coils, and removable sloped drain pans under AHRI 440 and UL 1995 listings.

Key points:
- Low-profile 10" to 11" casing depth fits tight soffits and guestroom entries
- 200 to 1,200 CFM concealed or cabinet configurations with quiet ECM motor
- 2-pipe and 4-pipe hydronic coil options with removable sloped drain pan
- AHRI 440 certified performance and UL 1995 listed assembly
