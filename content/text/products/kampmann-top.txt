Name: TOP Unit Heaters

Summary: TOP hydronic unit heaters deliver 5 to 110 kW (17,000 to 375,000 BTU/h) of warm-air heating for industrial halls, warehouses, workshops, and large high-bay interiors. Configurable for horizontal wall mounting or vertical ceiling mounting with KaMax air diffusers and 2-speed AC or 0–10V EC motors.

Key points:
- Broad 5 to 110 kW (17,000 to 375,000 BTU/h) hydronic output for large volumes
- Horizontal wall-mount or vertical ceiling-mount with directional KaMax air diffuser
- Available with 2-speed AC or stepless 0–10V EC fan motors
