Name: X-Plus UV-C Fixtures

Summary: An economical NEMA-4X UV-C fixture for 17" to 61" very high output lamps that mounts from the outside of an air handler, plenum, or duct through a 1" hole. Designed for both new construction and quick retrofit installations, allowing lamp and ballast service without entering the airstream.

Key points:
- NEMA-4X weather-resistant exterior enclosure
- Single 1" access hole for new and retrofit installation
- 17" to 61" very high output 254 nm germicidal lamps
- 120V–277V electronic ballast serviced from outside the unit
