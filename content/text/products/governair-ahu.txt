Name: Custom Air Handlers

Summary: Governair custom air handlers deliver 2,000 to 150,000+ CFM using 3" or 3.5" double-wall Integrated Frame (ITF) construction on welded structural tube base frames with injected foam (R-24) or fiberglass (R-12.5) insulation. Rated up to ±10" WC at L/240 deflection (<1% leakage at ±8" WC) with no thru-metal except fasteners, stacked air tunnels, optional third-layer standing seam roofs up to 39'-8" span, and Miami-Dade / OSHPD seismic certifications.

Key points:
- 3" or 3.5" ITF frame on welded structural tube base (R-24 injected foam or R-12.5 fiberglass; no thru-metal except fasteners)
- Low leakage (<1% at ±8" WC) and rigid L/200 to L/240 deflection up to ±10" WC with perforated acoustic liners
- Miami-Dade Intertek TAS-201/202/203, OSHPD seismic, third-layer standing seam roof up to 39'-8" span, and Rapid Knockdown
- Available with Gen IV FANWALL arrays (IE5+ or ECMi motors), slide-out coil racks, CHW/HW/DX/steam/gas heat, and BABA options
