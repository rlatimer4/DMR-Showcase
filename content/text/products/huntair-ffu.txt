Name: CleanPak Fan Filter Units

Summary: Fan filter units for cleanroom ceilings, with a backward-curved plenum fan and a high-efficiency brushless DC motor, plus monitoring and control for fab operation. CleanPak units can be supplied with a sensible cooling coil on the side, an arrangement sometimes called PlenPak.

Key points:
- High-efficiency EC motor
- Optional side-mounted sensible cooling coil (PlenPak)
- Central monitoring and control
- For semiconductor, pharmaceutical and research cleanrooms
