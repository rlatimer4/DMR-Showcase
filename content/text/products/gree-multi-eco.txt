Name: Multi ECO R32 Multi-Zone System

Summary: Multi ECO R-32 value multi-zone heat pump systems (18,000 to 36,000 BTU/h) condition 2 to 4 independent zones from a single outdoor unit at up to 21.0 SEER2. Built with low-GWP R-32 refrigerant, pre-charged manifold connections, and flexible indoor style pairings.

Key points:
- 18,000 to 36,000 BTU/h value multi-zone R-32 system serving 2 to 4 independent zones
- Up to 21.0 SEER2 efficiency with individual zone temperature control
- Low-GWP R-32 refrigerant with simplified pre-charged manifold connections
