Name: CRC Sensible Cooling Series Fan Powered VAV Terminals

Summary: Series-flow fan-powered VAV terminals with a sensible cooling coil, in a standard-height cabinet. Paired with a dedicated outdoor air system that removes the moisture, they cool each zone with a dry coil, an arrangement often called a DOAS VAV.

Key points:
- Standard-height cabinet
- Sensible (dry) cooling coil at the zone
- Works with DOAS and low-temp desiccant air handlers
