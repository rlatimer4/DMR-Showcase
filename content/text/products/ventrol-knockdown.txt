Name: Knockdown Air Handlers

Summary: Ventrol field or palletized knockdown air handlers ship with 2" (±8" WC @ L/200) or 4" (±12" WC @ L/240) smooth-exterior structural panels and components already disassembled on pallets for rigging into tight mechanical rooms. Built on welded structural tube base frames with a standard 2" upturned floor lip, washdown sealing, modified thermal break or PolyBlock True No Thru Metal, and optional all-aluminum construction.

Key points:
- Field or palletized knockdown: unit components ship already disassembled on pallets
- 4" (±12" WC L/240) or 2" (±8" WC L/200) smooth-exterior structural panelized construction
- Modified thermal break or PolyBlock True No Thru Metal; polyurethane spray or injected foam
- Welded structural tube base, 2" upturned floor lip, washdown, perf liners & all-aluminum option
