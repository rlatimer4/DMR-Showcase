Name: Electric Baseboard

Summary: Sterling commercial electric baseboard and finned-tube heaters deliver 187 to 250 W/ft across 2 ft to 10 ft lengths (500W to 2,500W) in 120V, 208V, 240V, and 277V single-phase.
