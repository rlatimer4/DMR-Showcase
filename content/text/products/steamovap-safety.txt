Name: Safety Controls

Summary: Hardwired high-limit humidistats, airflow proving switches, and water leak detectors that shut down or modulate the humidifier before duct condensation occurs. Essential protection for air handlers and distribution manifolds across commercial buildings.

Key points:
- Modulating and cutout duct high-limit humidistats (85% RH limit)
- Paddle and electronic airflow proving switches
- Water leak detectors wired to hardwired humidifier safety interlocks
