Name: Baseboard HK

Summary: Fan-assisted hydronic perimeter heating and cooling baseboard units combining a whisper-quiet EC tangential fan with a high-output copper-aluminum convector for curtain-wall and high-load perimeters.

Key points:
- Whisper-quiet EC tangential fan delivers high perimeter heating and cooling output from a compact floor profile
- Available in 2-pipe or 4-pipe configurations with an integral stainless steel condensate tray for sensible or condensing cooling
- Custom made-to-measure heavy-gauge enclosures accommodate mitred corners and structural column cut-outs
