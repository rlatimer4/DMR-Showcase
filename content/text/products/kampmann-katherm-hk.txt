Name: Katherm HK Trench Heating and Cooling

Summary: Katherm HK perimeter floor trench units (290 or 360 mm wide × 135 or 190 mm deep) deliver 2-pipe changeover or 4-pipe hydronic heating alongside sensible and latent cooling at floor-to-ceiling glass. Quiet 0–10V EC tangential fans, integral condensate pans, mitred corners, and custom curves integrate seamlessly into glazing lines.

Key points:
- 290 or 360 mm × 135 or 190 mm floor trench with integral stainless condensate pan
- 2-pipe changeover or 4-pipe hydronic heating plus sensible and latent cooling
- Quiet EC tangential fan with 0–10V control, custom lengths, mitres, and curves
