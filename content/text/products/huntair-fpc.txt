Name: Huntair FPC Air Handler

Summary: Huntair FPC (Frame & Panel Construction) custom air handlers combine 2-5/8" injected polyurethane foam double-wall panels with extruded aluminum framing, an epoxy true thermal break (no thru-metal including fasteners), and gullwing clip fasteners on welded tube bases. Rated to ultra-rigid L/500 casing deflection at ±8" to ±10" WC, FPC units deliver full powder coating (antimicrobial/FDA), washdown capability, and rapid knockdown assembly.

Key points:
- 2-5/8" injected polyurethane foam double-wall panels in extruded aluminum frame with epoxy true thermal break (no fastener thru-metal)
- Ultra-rigid L/500 casing deflection rating at ±8" to ±10" WC static pressure for cleanroom, pharma, and hospital suites
- Gullwing clip fasteners make factory or field knockdown assembly and future panel replacement fast and repeatable
- Full interior and exterior powder coating with antimicrobial and FDA options; washdown construction with solid double-wall liner
