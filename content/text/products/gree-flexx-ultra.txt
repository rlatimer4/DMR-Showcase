Name: FLEXX Ultra R32 Central Heat Pump

Summary: FLEXX Ultra R-32 central ducted cold-climate heat pumps (2 to 5 tons) pair a side-discharge inverter outdoor unit with a multi-positional air handler up to 1.0 in. w.g. ESP. Its EVI inverter compressor delivers 100% heating capacity at 5°F and continues heating down to −22°F (−30°C).

Key points:
- 2 to 5 tons cold-climate R-32 central ducted split heat pump system
- EVI inverter compressor maintains 100% heat at 5°F and heats down to −22°F (−30°C)
- Side-discharge outdoor unit with multi-positional AHU up to 1.0 in. w.g. ESP
