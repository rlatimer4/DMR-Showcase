Name: Make-Up Air Units

Summary: Rapid Engineering 4000-Series welded-frame (up to 150,000 CFM) and 2000-Series mechanical-frame (up to 40,000 CFM) industrial direct-fired and indirect/hydronic make-up air handlers deliver 100% OA, VAV, economizer, or 80/20 recirculating air with optional supplemental cooling and energy recovery.

Key points:
- 4000-Series welded-frame industrial direct-fired AHUs up to 150,000 CFM; 2000-Series mechanical-frame units up to 40,000 CFM
- Direct-fired gas line burners deliver up to 92% thermal efficiency with 25:1 to 30:1 modulation turndown
- Vertical, horizontal, indoor, or outdoor configurations for 100% OA, VAV, economizer, or RAM-M 80/20 recirculating duty
- Optional supplemental DX/CHW cooling and energy recovery integrate with heavy-duty G-90 galvanized steel housings
