Name: FANWALL Systems

Summary: Ventrol Gen IV FANWALL systems deliver N+1 and N-2 redundant direct-drive fan arrays in aluminum, galvanized, or stainless cubes engineered for elevators (85"H x 48"W) and tight retrofits like Virginia Hospital Center (two 65,000 CFM 12-fan arrays holding 4" duct static down to 10% flow). Available with Coplanar Silencers, Passive Noise Control (PNC), motorized isolation dampers, and IE5+ or ultra-low-harmonic VFDs.

Key points:
- Proven live-hospital retrofit design (e.g., VHC Arlington dual 65,000 CFM 12-fan N-2 arrays rigged via 85"H x 48"W elevator)
- Maintains high static pressure (4"+ WC down to 10% flow) with N+1 / N-2 redundancy and System Optimization Controls
- Coplanar Silencers plus optional inlet Passive Noise Control (PNC) and knockdown discharge attenuators
- Zero-System-Effect backdraft dampers, motorized discharge isolation dampers, IE5+ / ultra-low harmonic VFDs, AMCA & UL 1995/CSA
