Name: Plate Heat Exchangers

Summary: Klingenburg PWT cross-flow and counter-flow plate heat exchangers recover sensible heat while keeping supply and exhaust airstreams completely isolated with zero moving parts.

Key points:
- Sealed aluminum, epoxy-coated or stainless steel PWT transfer plates prevent airstream cross-contamination
- Cross-flow and high-efficiency counter-flow cores up to 85% sensible effectiveness
- No moving parts for minimal maintenance; available with integrated face-and-bypass dampers
- Modular block stacking suits laboratories, hospitals and custom air handlers
