Name: GMV MTAC

Summary: Concealed 12,000 BTU/h Mini-VRF condensing unit engineered to slide directly into a standard 42" x 16" PTAC wall sleeve and serve one or two whisper-quiet VRF indoor units in hotels, multifamily, and historic retrofits.

Key points:
- Replaces noisy through-the-wall PTAC units using the existing 42" x 16" wall sleeve with no exterior facade changes
- Connects in 1:1 or 2:1 configurations to GREE VRF high-wall, concealed duct, cassette, or floor-console indoor units
- Inverter rotary compressor achieves up to 15.0 SEER2 with cooling to 118°F and low-ambient heating down to −4°F
- CAN-bus communication uses non-shielded wiring for reliable BAS integration and hotel keycard control
