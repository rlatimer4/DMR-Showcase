Name: Rotary Heat Exchangers

Summary: Klingenburg RRT, RRS, RRU, and RRA-sealed rotary heat exchangers (energy recovery wheels) engineered for commercial AHUs, custom penthouses, and DOAS units, featuring pressure-washable DekaTru™ coating technology with low air friction and high heat/moisture transfer.

Key points:
- Available in RRT (Premium), RRS (Classic cassette), and RRU (Standard) rotor housings with RRA contact seals
- DekaTru™ coating delivers high heat/moisture transfer, low air friction, and industry-leading adhesion that withstands pressure washing
- Matrix options cover sensible aluminum (P), Silikagel enthalpy (E), epoxy-coated (K), and 3Å molecular-sieve sorption (N)
- Purge sector design limits exhaust-to-supply carryover to <0.1% at up to 85% efficiency; segmented wheels to 20 ft diameter

Specifications:
- Airflow & size: 500 to 120,000+ CFM; rotor diameters from 600 mm to 6,000 mm (2 ft to 20 ft); RRT, RRS & RRU housings w/ RRA seals
- Media & coating: Sensible aluminum, Silikagel enthalpy, epoxy, 3Å zeolite, and pressure-washable DekaTru™ coating
- Efficiency: Up to 80–85% sensible & latent recovery with purge sector (<0.1% EATR)
- Certifications: AHRI 1060 and Eurovent certified performance
