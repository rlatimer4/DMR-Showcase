Name: SGX and SSX Single Duct Retrofit Terminals

Summary: SGX and SSX slide-in single-duct VAV retrofit terminals handle 100 to 5,000 CFM with round, oval, or rectangular slip-and-drive inlets. Designed for fast pneumatic-to-DDC or dual-duct conversions using factory-mounted Belimo actuators and multi-point flow rings under AHRI 880.

Key points:
- 100 to 5,000 CFM slide-in VAV retrofit with round, oval, or rectangular inlets
- Ideal for fast pneumatic-to-DDC and dual-duct terminal conversions
- Factory-mounted Belimo actuator with multi-point averaging flow ring
- AHRI 880 rated performance and UL 1995 listed
