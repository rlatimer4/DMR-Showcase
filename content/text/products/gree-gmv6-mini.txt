Name: GMV6 Ultra Heat Mini Heat Pump

Summary: GMV6 Ultra Heat Mini VRF outdoor units provide 3, 4, and 5 tons (36,000 to 60,000 BTU/h) on 208/230V single-phase service with cold-climate EVI heating down to −22°F (−30°C). Supports up to 984 ft of piping and connects up to 9 indoor units for light commercial and residential projects.

Key points:
- 3, 4, and 5 tons (36k–60k BTU/h) 208/230V single-phase compact side-discharge cabinet
- EVI Ultra Heat technology sustains heating operation down to −22°F (−30°C)
- Up to 984 ft total piping connects as many as 9 VRF indoor units
- AHRI 1230 / 210/240 certified and ETL listed
