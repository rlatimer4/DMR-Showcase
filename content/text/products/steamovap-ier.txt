Name: IER Electric Steam Humidifier

Summary: Industrial and commercial electric steam humidifier using immersed Incoloy resistive heating elements in a cleanable stainless steel tank to deliver 6 to 370 lb/h of sterile, chemical-free steam.

Key points:
- Steam output 6 to 370 lb/h (2.8 to 168 kg/h) per unit with ±1% RH proportional modulation accuracy
- Cleanable 304L stainless steel insulated evaporation cylinder eliminates disposable plastic bottles
- Operates reliably on potable city water, softened water, reverse osmosis (RO), or deionized (DI) water
- Color touch-screen PLC includes native BACnet MS/TP, BACnet IP, and Modbus communications
