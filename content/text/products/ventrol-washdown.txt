Name: Washdown Air Handlers

Summary: Ventrol washdown air handlers pair smooth-exterior 2" (R-16 foam) or 4" (R-32 foam; ±12" WC @ L/240) structural panelized construction with continuously welded 1-1/2" to 2" upturned stainless/aluminum floors, caulked internal/external seams, floor drains in every section, liquid-tight conduit, and sprayed non-slip Polyurea membrane coatings for high-pressure sanitation.

Key points:
- Continuously welded floor seams turned up 1-1/2" to 2" with floor drains in every section and liquid-tight conduit
- Panel seams caulked internally and externally plus cover plates/gasketing and sprayed non-slip Polyurea membrane option
- PolyBlock True No Thru Metal (1" PolyBlock strips eliminate fastener thermal bridge) with R-32 injected/spray polyurethane foam
- Stainless steel or all-aluminum skins, perforated cleanable acoustic liners, marine viewports, and palletized knockdown
