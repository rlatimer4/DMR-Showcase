Name: V-Cube Slim Vertical Self-Contained Systems

Summary: Mammoth V-Cube Slim cataloged floor-by-floor VAV self-contained units span 13 sizes from 15 to 90 tons (5,000 to 24,100 CFM), reaching up to 16.1 EER and 4.3 COP heating. Engineered with variable-speed lead compressors, CNC tube bending, and direct-drive FANWALL fans, narrow <36" doorway modules—including 70–90 ton split-width units—fit inside a 57" deep freight elevator without refrigeration breaks.

Key points:
- 13 sizes from 15 to 90 tons (5,000–24,100 CFM) achieving up to 16.1 EER cooling and 4.3 COP heat-pump efficiency
- Cataloged <36" doorway knockdown design; 70–90 ton split-width unit fits into a 57" deep freight elevator with zero refrigerant breaks
- Variable-speed lead compressors, CNC tube bending, shell-and-tube (cooling) or brazed-plate (WSHP) HX, and waterside economizer
- Direct-drive FANWALL supply fans with optional Coplanar Silencers, DX/CW/HW/WiSE coils, and remote electric heat control

Specifications:
- Capacity & efficiency: 13 sizes from 15 to 90 tons (5,000–24,100 CFM); up to 16.1 EER cooling and 4.3 COP heating

- Airside & options: Direct-drive FANWALL w/ Coplanar Silencer, waterside economizer (WiSE), DX/CW/HW coils, remote electric heat control
