Name: ACA Atomizing Humidifier

Summary: ACA dual-fluid compressed-air and water atomizing humidifiers and coolers produce a 2 to 4 µm uniform dry fog through self-cleaning stainless steel resonator nozzles. Each nozzle delivers 3.3 to 18.7 lb/h from a modular wall control cabinet with dual pressure regulators for duct or space mounting.

Key points:
- Self-cleaning stainless steel resonator nozzles emit 2 to 4 µm dry fog mist
- 3.3 to 18.7 lb/h per nozzle for both direct-space and in-duct humidification/cooling
- Modular wall control cabinet includes dual water and compressed-air regulators
