Name: VRF Ducted Fan Coils

Summary: Concealed ducted VRF indoor units from 5,000 to 96,000 BTU/h span slim 7.9" low-static models up to 0.80–1.0 in. w.g. high-static fan coils. Factory-equipped with an integral condensate lift pump and electronic expansion valve for hidden above-ceiling ductwork.

Key points:
- 5,000 to 96,000 BTU/h across slim low-static, medium-static, and high-static series
- External static pressure up to 0.80 in. w.g. (up to 1.0 in. w.g. on high-static models)
- Compact 7.9" height on low-profile units with integral condensate pump and EEV
