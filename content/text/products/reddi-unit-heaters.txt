Name: Electric Unit and Wall Heaters

Summary: Suspended industrial and commercial electric unit heaters, hazardous-location heaters and architectural wall/cabinet heaters engineered for reliable spot and space heating.

Key points:
- Explosion-proof hazardous-location unit heaters alongside commercial suspended models
- Heavy-duty commercial and residential wall heaters with enclosed finned tubular elements
- Available from 1.5 kW to 60 kW across 120V–600V single- and three-phase power
- UL 2021 / CSA listed with integral contactors, transformers and built-in thermostats
