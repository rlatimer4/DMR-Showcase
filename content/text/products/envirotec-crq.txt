Name: CRQ Series Flow Fan Powered VAV Terminals

Summary: CRQ series-flow fan-powered VAV terminal units deliver 200 to 3,800 CFM with integral tuned discharge and plenum attenuation for NC-25 to NC-30 acoustic spaces. Variable-speed ECM motors maintain constant zone discharge airflow with hot water or electric reheat options under AHRI 880 certification.

Key points:
- Series fan-powered operation from 200 to 3,800 CFM for constant zone discharge
- Integral tuned discharge and plenum attenuator for NC-25 to NC-30 acoustics
- Variable-speed ECM fan motor with hot water or electric reheat options
- AHRI 880 certified radiated and discharge sound, UL 1995 listed
