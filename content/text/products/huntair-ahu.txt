Name: Smooth-Exterior Custom Air Handlers

Summary: Huntair Smooth-Exterior custom air handlers deliver 1,500 to 200,000+ CFM using 2" (R-16 injected foam / R-8.3 fiberglass; ±8" WC @ L/200) or 4" (R-32 injected foam / R-16.7 fiberglass; ±12" WC @ L/240, up to 0.5% leakage) structural panelized construction on welded tube bases. Options include PolyBlock True No Thru Metal (including fasteners), full powder coating (antimicrobial/FDA), 2" upturned floor lip, washdown, third-layer standing seam roof up to 39'-8", and field/palletized knockdown.

Key points:
- Smooth-exterior 4" (R-32 foam; ±12" WC @ L/240, to 0.5% leakage) or 2" (R-16 foam; ±8" WC @ L/200) panelized casing on welded tube base
- Modified thermal break or PolyBlock True No Thru Metal option (1" PolyBlock strips eliminate fastener thermal bridging)
- Full interior/exterior powder coat (antimicrobial/FDA), 2" upturned floor lip, washdown construction, and standing seam roof to 39'-8"
- Field or palletized knockdown shipping, stacked air tunnels, cantilevered/bolt-on catwalks, OSHPD seismic, and BABA options
