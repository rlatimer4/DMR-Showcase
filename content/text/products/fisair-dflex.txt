Name: DFLEX Dehumidifiers

Summary: Large-capacity modular desiccant air handling dehumidifiers delivering 7,500 to 24,000 m³/h (~4,400 to 14,125 CFM) and up to 152 kg/h of moisture extraction in an EN 1886 thermal-break cabinet. Backed by Fisair's 55+ years in humidity control, 2-year standard warranty, and Fisair Selection Tool (FST) application software.

Key points:
- High drying capacity from 51 to 152 kg/h (112 to 335 lb/h) across ~4,400 to 14,125 CFM process airflow
- Configurable modular AHU sections combine mixing boxes, MERV/HEPA filtration, pre/post cooling, and silica-gel rotors
- Rigid EN 1886 Class T2/TB2 double-wall thermal-break panels minimize casing air leakage and condensation
- Selected via FST (Fisair Selection Tool) with factory engineering support, dependable lead times, and a 2-year warranty
