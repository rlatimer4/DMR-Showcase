Name: Livo R32 Mini-Split

- 9,000 to 36,000 BTU/h single-zone inverter mini-split with low-GWP R-32 refrigerant
- Up to 20.5 SEER2 and 10.0 HSPF2 efficiency with quiet G10 variable-speed compressor
- Continuous heating down to −13°F (−25°C) with 115V options in 9k and 12k sizes
