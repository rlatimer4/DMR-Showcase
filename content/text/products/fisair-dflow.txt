Name: DFLOW Dehumidifiers

Summary: Dual-rotor ultra-low dew point desiccant dehumidifiers engineered for lithium-ion battery dry rooms, pharmaceutical suites, and microelectronics manufacturing down to −70°C (−94°F) dew point with faster setpoint recovery and lower OPEX. Backed by 55+ years in humidity control, FST selection software, and a 2-year standard warranty.

Key points:
- Dual-rotor cascade architecture reaches ultra-low supply dew points down to −70°C (−94°F) CDP with faster setpoint recovery
- Energy-recovery purge sector dramatically cuts reactivation energy and operating cost compared to single-wheel dry-room units
- Double-wall thermal-break vapor-tight construction prevents ambient moisture infiltration into dry rooms
- Selected via FST (Fisair Selection Tool) with BACnet/Modbus PLC/HMI controls and a 2-year standard warranty
