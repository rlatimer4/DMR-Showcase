Name: VB / VR Vertical Reduced Footprint Blower Coil Units

Summary: Reduced-footprint vertical blower coil units up to 3,000 CFM engineered for tight mechanical closets, stair-core chases, and retrofits where floor area is constrained.

Key points:
- Compact vertical footprint fits mechanical closets immediately adjacent to occupied classrooms or offices
- Capacities up to 3,000 CFM with 4- to 8-row chilled water, hot water, or DX coils and MERV 8–14 filters
- Double-wall insulated interior with sloped stainless steel drain pan option for IAQ compliance
- AHRI 430 / 440 certified and UL 1995 listed for commercial and institutional retrofits
