Name: Pneumatic Controls

Summary: Factory-calibrated pneumatic VAV control packages operating on 15 to 25 psig control air (direct-acting or reverse-acting) for legacy pneumatic control buildings. Combines a calibrated pneumatic velocity controller and damper actuator as a direct drop-in replacement for existing zones.

Key points:
- Direct drop-in replacement for legacy pneumatic VAV zones (15 to 25 psig air)
- Available in both direct-acting and reverse-acting control configurations
- Includes factory-calibrated pneumatic velocity controller and damper actuator
