Name: HP Horizontal High Performance Fan Coil Units

Summary: Horizontal high-performance fan coil units from 600 to 2,400 CFM in exposed and concealed cabinets, featuring sloped IAQ drain pans, removable fan decks, and quiet acoustic performance.

Key points:
- High-capacity 600 to 2,400 CFM airflow range bridges standard fan coils and commercial blower coils
- Exposed, concealed free-return, and plenum-return cabinets with rear or bottom return options
- Available in 2-pipe or 4-pipe hydronic coil configurations with factory-assembled valve piping packages
- Removable fan deck and double-sloped IAQ drain pan support AHRI 440 performance and easy servicing
