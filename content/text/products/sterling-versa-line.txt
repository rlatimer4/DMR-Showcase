Name: Versa-Line Finned Tube

Summary: Versa-Line is Sterling's versatile commercial finned-tube enclosure system offering 7.5" to 24" slope-top and flat-top cabinets in 18+ baked powder-coat colors. Pairs 3/4", 1", or 1-1/4" copper/aluminum elements for hydronic or low-pressure steam perimeter heating.

Key points:
- 7.5" to 24" slope-top and flat-top commercial finned-tube enclosure styles
- Supports 3/4", 1", and 1-1/4" copper/aluminum hydronic or low-pressure steam elements
- Finished in 18+ standard baked powder-coat architectural colors
