Name: Steam Distributing Coils

Summary: Freeze-resistant Nortek steam distributing coils pair a 1" outer condensing tube with a concentric 5/8" inner steam-distributing tube to heat outdoor air evenly. Resists freeze damage on sub-zero preheat coils from 2 to 150 psig modulating steam under AHRI 410 certification.

Key points:
- Tube-in-tube 1" outer / 5/8" concentric inner design spreads steam evenly
- Maintains uniform face temperature and resists freeze damage on sub-zero OA
- Operates on 2 to 150 psig modulating steam; AHRI 410 certified performance
