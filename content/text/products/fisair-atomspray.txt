Name: AtomSpray

Summary: AtomSpray dual-fluid humidifiers and adiabatic coolers blend potable or RO water with oil-free compressed air to generate an ultra-fine 5 to 10 µm mist for rapid, drip-free evaporation. Configurable for direct-space or in-duct installation to humidify and cool demanding industrial spaces.

Key points:
- Dual-fluid compressed-air and water spray heads for in-duct or direct-space mounting
- Ultra-fine 5 to 10 µm droplets evaporate rapidly without wetting nearby surfaces
- Operates on 1–4 bar potable/RO water and 2.5–4 bar oil-free compressed air
