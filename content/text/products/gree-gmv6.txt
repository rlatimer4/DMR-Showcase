Name: GMV6 Heat Pump and Heat Recovery VRF

Summary: GMV6 variable refrigerant flow outdoor units span 6 to 48 tons (72,000 to 576,000 BTU/h) in 208/230V or 460V three-phase 2-pipe heat pump and 3-pipe heat recovery models. Driven by all-DC inverter variable-speed scroll compressors with CAN-bus communications under AHRI 1230.

Key points:
- 6 to 48 tons (72k–576k BTU/h) in 208/230V or 460V 3-phase modular configurations
- Available in 2-pipe Heat Pump or 3-pipe simultaneous Heat Recovery VRF
- All-DC inverter scroll compressors with CAN-bus communication; AHRI 1230 certified
