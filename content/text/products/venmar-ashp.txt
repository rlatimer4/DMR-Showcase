Name: Standard and Low-Ambient Air-Source Heat Pumps

Summary: Venmar CES EnergyPack air-source heat pumps span 25 to 120 tons using R-454B in 3" ITF double-wall cabinets (R-24 foam; ±10" WC @ L/240) for mixed-air or 100% DOAS with integral energy recovery. Sustaining mechanical heating down to 5°F ambient (−10°F SST) or −20°F ambient (−40°F SST) with Liquid Injection at COPs of 2.0–4.5, units are configurable for rooftop or indoor elevator-knockdown installation.

Key points:
- 25 to 120 tons EnergyPack ASHP DOAS with R-454B and typical heating COPs of 2.0 to 4.5 (up to 4.1 @ 47°F; 1.7 @ −20°F ambient)
- Optional Liquid Injection lowers minimum compressor operating ambient to −20°F (−40°F SST), reducing electric/gas backup demand
- Indoor, elevator-knockdown, or rooftop configurations with integral energy recovery wheel/plate and Vee ACCU 31" MaEr EC condenser fans
- 3" ITF double-wall construction (±10" WC @ L/240), NASDA-integrated FLA/MCA/MOP data, FANWALL arrays, and factory BACnet DDC
