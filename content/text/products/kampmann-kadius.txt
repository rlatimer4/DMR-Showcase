Name: KaDius Ceiling Cassette Fan Coils

Summary: KaDius circular hydronic ceiling cassettes, for exposed or recessed ceiling mounting, are styled as an architectural ceiling feature delivering 360° radial air distribution with quiet EC fan operation. Available with 2-pipe or 4-pipe cooling and heating coils plus an integral condensate pump for open-ceiling offices, retail, and restaurants.

Key points:
- Architectural circular ceiling cassette fan coil with 360° radial discharge
- 2-pipe or 4-pipe hydronic cooling and heating with quiet EC radial fan
- Integral condensate pump suited to open-ceiling offices, showrooms, and retail
