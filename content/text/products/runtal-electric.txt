Name: Electric Radiators

Summary: Electric flat-tube steel panel radiators, baseboards and designer Omnipanel warmers delivering silent radiant and convective perimeter comfort where hydronic piping cannot reach.

Key points:
- Sealed heat-transfer fluid core provides even surface warmth with zero fan noise or exposed hot elements
- Baseboard, vertical wall panel and Omnipanel architectural warmer configurations
- 500W to 3,000W capacities across 120V–277V with optional integral programmable thermostats
- UL listed and offered in Runtal's full architectural powder-coat color range
