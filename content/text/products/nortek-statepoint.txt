Name: StatePoint Liquid Cooling

Summary: Nortek Data Center Cooling's StatePoint Liquid Cooling (SPLC) uses a patented hydrophobic microporous membrane exchanger to chill facility water via indirect evaporation — keeping water completely separated from the airstream with zero droplet carryover, no refrigerants and no glycol.

Key points:
- Microporous membrane heat exchanger prevents water carryover, scaling or outdoor contaminants in the facility loop
- Refrigerant-free and glycol-free indirect evaporative cooling with EC FANWALL array
- Cuts cooling power use by 20–30% (supporting sub-1.19 design PUEs) and reduces water consumption by up to 90% in cool climates vs. cooling towers
- Supplies cold water cleanly to CoilWall fan walls, Rear Door Heat Exchangers (RDHx) and liquid-cooling CDUs
