Name: FANWALL Systems

Summary: Developed by Huntair—creator of FANWALL Technology—Gen IV FANWALL arrays deliver N+1 redundancy and uniform coil face velocity using 12" to 27" wheels balanced to AMCA 204-96 Class BV-5 Grade G.55 (0.022 in/s peak). Available with IE5+ Ultra-Premium ferrite-magnet motors, space-saving FANWALL EC (12"–24" shorter), Zero-System-Effect dampers, Coplanar Silencers, and System Optimization Controls.

Key points:
- Original patented Gen IV FANWALL array dynamically balanced to AMCA 204-96 Class BV-5 Grade G.55 (0.022 in/s peak)
- 12" to 27" wheels with 1–15 HP (20/30 HP on 27") incremental induction, IE5+ Ultra-Premium (200k+ hr L10), or ECMi motors
- Zero-System-Effect backdraft dampers, System Optimization Controls for peak FEI, and FANWALL EC (saves 12"–24" length)
- Coplanar Silencers attenuate airborne noise; AMCA 211/311, UL 1995 listed, and OSHPD pre-approved seismic
