Name: KaDeck Fan Coils

Summary: A low-profile ceiling-mounted fan coil featuring hybrid sheet-steel and molded EPP construction at roughly 165 mm (6.5") in height for new and retrofit offices. Delivers quiet, draft-free 1-way, 2-way, or 360° Coanda air discharge in 2-pipe or 4-pipe hydronic systems.

Key points:
- Low ~165 mm (6.5") installed height for shallow ceiling plenums
- Lightweight hybrid galvanized steel and acoustic EPP casing
- Draft-free 1-way, 2-way, or 360° Coanda-effect air distribution
- Available in 2-pipe and 4-pipe hydronic configurations
