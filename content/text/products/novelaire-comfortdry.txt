Name: ComfortDRY Packaged Dehumidification Units

Summary: NovelAire ComfortDRY (DES/DX & DES/HYD) packaged desiccant DOAS and humidity-control units span 300 to 15,000 CFM (custom to 25,000 CFM) to deliver 35°F–45°F supply dew points at neutral dry-bulb temperatures. Using proprietary 4A molecular-sieve ECW wheels, R-454B scrolls, and 115–125°F condenser-reclaim regeneration (105–145°F DES/HYD), ComfortDry+ adds an ERV and third regen tunnel for up to 100% OA and cooling-only mode.

Key points:
- ComfortDRY DES/DX (300–15,000 CFM; custom 25,000 CFM) & DES/HYD (1,000–15,000 CFM) hold 35°F–45°F dewpoints at neutral dry bulb
- Condenser-reclaim regeneration at 115–125°F (110–120°F w/ ComfortDry+ ERV; 105–145°F w/ hot water on DES/HYD) eliminates overcool/reheat penalties
- ComfortDry+ adds an ERV wheel and dedicated third regen tunnel for up to 100% OA and cooling-only mode for multipurpose buildings
- Proprietary 4A molecular-sieve ECW wheel, R-454B scrolls, 2" R-13 foam casing, SelectDRY sizing to ASHRAE 0.4% design day, BACnet HMI

Specifications:
- Models & airflow: ComfortDRY DES/DX (300–15,000 CFM; custom 25,000 CFM) & DES/HYD (1,000–15,000 CFM hydronic); up to 20% OA std or 100% OA w/ ERV
- Dew point & regen: 35–45°F discharge dew point; 115–125°F (110–120°F ComfortDry+ ERV) internal R-454B condenser reclaim, or 105–145°F hot water

- Selection & controls: Sized to ASHRAE 0.4% design day via SelectDRY software; ECM/VFD fans, speed-controlled wheels, BACnet/HMI
