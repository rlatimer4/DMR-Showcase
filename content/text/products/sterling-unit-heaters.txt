Name: Steam and Hot Water Unit Heaters

Summary: Sterling horizontal- and vertical-discharge propeller unit heaters deliver 15,000 to 610,000 BTU/h on hot water (up to 150 psig / 375°F) or steam (2 to 150 psig) with copper or steel coils. Rugged construction handles mechanical rooms, loading docks, warehouses, greenhouses, and parking garages.

Key points:
- Broad 15,000 to 610,000 BTU/h horizontal- and vertical-discharge range
- Handles hot water (up to 150 psig / 375°F) or steam (2 to 150 psig) coils
- Built for warehouses, loading docks, mechanical rooms, and greenhouses
