Name: Energy Conservation Wheels (ECW)

Summary: NovelAire Energy Conservation Wheels (ECW) cover 300 to 60,000+ CFM in slide-out cassettes or segmented OEM frames using selective 3Å molecular-sieve desiccant-coated aluminum media. AHRI 1060 certified with adjustable purge sectors limiting exhaust cross-contamination (EATR) to <0.04%–0.1%.

Key points:
- 300 to 60,000+ CFM slide-out cassettes and segmented OEM total-recovery wheels
- 3Å molecular-sieve coating on aluminum selectively transfers moisture without VOCs
- Adjustable purge sector restricts EATR exhaust carryover to <0.04%–0.1%
- AHRI 1060 certified effectiveness and UL 900 Class 1 rated
