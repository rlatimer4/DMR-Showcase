Name: Standard and Low-Ambient Air-Source Heat Pumps

Summary: Mammoth standard and low-ambient commercial packaged air-source heat pumps span 25 to 120 tons on the UltraLine platform using low-GWP R-454B in 3" ITF double-wall cabinets (R-24 foam; ±10" WC @ L/240). Variable-speed and digital scroll compressors sustain mechanical heating down to 5°F ambient (−10°F SST), while optional Liquid Injection extends reliable heat down to −20°F ambient (−40°F SST) at COPs up to 4.1 (1.7 at −20°F).

Key points:
- 25 to 120 tons UltraLine ASHP platform with R-454B achieving typical heating COPs of 2.0 to 4.5 (1.7 at −20°F up to 4.1 at 47°F OA)
- 5°F ambient (−10°F SST) with VSC/digital scroll; optional Liquid Injection extends compressor heating down to −20°F ambient (−40°F SST)
- Redundant Vee ACCU microchannel kits (105°F design ambient, 31" MaEr EC condenser fans, VFD compressors to 40 tons/ckt)
- 3" ITF double-wall cabinet (±10" WC @ L/240), NASDA-integrated FLA/MCA/MOP electrical data, defrost electric heat, and ER wheels/cores

Specifications:
- Capacity & climate: 25 to 120 tons UltraLine platform; R-454B; mechanical heating to 5°F ambient (−10°F SST) or −20°F ambient (−40°F SST) w/ Liquid Injection
- Efficiency & compressors: Typical COP 2.0–4.5 (4.1 @ 47°F, 3.0 @ 17°F, 2.5 @ 5°F, 2.0 @ −10°F, 1.7 @ −20°F); On-Off, 2-Step, Digital, VSC, or Liquid Injection
- Casing & ACCU: 3" ITF double-wall on welded tube base (±10" WC @ L/240); Vee ACCU kits w/ 31" MaEr EC fans & 105°F ambient rating; 2" Polyurea floor option
- Controls & options: NASDA-calculated FLA/MCA/MOP electrical data, supplemental/defrost electric heat, integral ER wheel/plate, FANWALL
