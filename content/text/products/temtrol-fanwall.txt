Name: FANWALL Systems

Summary: Arrays of smaller direct-drive fans that replace one large fan. Used to replace ageing fans in existing air handlers, they avoid the cost and disruption of a failure and can cut fan energy by as much as 40%.

Key points:
- Redundancy: remaining fans keep running if one fails
- Even airflow across coils and filters
- Fan cubes pass through a standard doorway for retrofit
