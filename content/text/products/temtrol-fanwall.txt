Name: FANWALL Systems

Summary: Temtrol Gen IV FANWALL systems replace aging DWDI and belt-drive centrifugal fans with 2 to 32+ direct-drive cube cells fitting 36" doors for live-building retrofits like 777 North Capitol St. (161,000 CFM). Balanced to AMCA 204-96 Class BV-5 Grade G.55 (0.022 in/s peak) with IE5+ or ECMi motors, Zero-System-Effect dampers, Coplanar Silencers, and System Optimization Controls.

Key points:
- Gen IV modular arrays (2 to 32+ cubes; e.g., 161,000 CFM 32-fan supply / 16-fan return retrofit at 777 N. Capitol St.)
- Factory run-tested & balanced to AMCA 204-96 Class BV-5 Grade G.55 (0.022 in/s peak); Zero-System-Effect backdraft dampers
- Incremental VFD induction, IE5+ Ultra-Premium (200k+ hr L10, ceramic bearings), or FANWALL EC (saves 12"–24" unit length)
- Coplanar Silencers absorb low-frequency noise while System Optimization Controls stage fans for peak FEI

- Configuration: Gen IV custom or retrofit FANWALL array from 2 to 32+ cube cells passing through 36" doors
