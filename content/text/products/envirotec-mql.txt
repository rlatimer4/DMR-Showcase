Name: MQL Indoor Air Handling Units

Summary: Flexible modular indoor air handling units spanning 600 to 10,000 CFM in horizontal or vertical configurations with single-wall or 1" double-wall construction, hinged access doors, and wide coil and filtration flexibility.

Key points:
- Modular indoor sections deliver 600 to 10,000 CFM in horizontal or vertical configurations for tight mechanical rooms
- Available in single-wall or 1" double-wall construction with easy-clean interior surfaces and hinged access doors
- Configurable with mixing boxes, MERV 8–14 filters, CHW/HW/DX/steam/electric coils, and VFD plenum fans
- AHRI 430 / 1350 certified performance and UL 1995 safety listed
