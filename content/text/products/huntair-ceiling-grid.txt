Name: Clean-Trak Modular Cleanroom Ceiling Grids

Summary: Clean-Trak heavy-duty extruded aluminum modular cleanroom ceiling grids seal ISO Class 3–8 environments with flush gasketed dry-seal or fluid gel-seal channels. Structurally supports CleanPak FFUs, filters, teardown lighting, sprinklers, utility drops, and walkable plenums.

Key points:
- Heavy-duty extruded aluminum modular grid for ISO Class 3–8 cleanrooms
- Available in flush gasketed dry-seal or fluid gel-seal ceiling channel profiles
- Supports CleanPak FFUs, walkable plenums, lighting, sprinklers, and utility drops
