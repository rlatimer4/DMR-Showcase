Name: VF Vertical Floor Mounted Fan Coil Units

Summary: Floor-mounted vertical perimeter fan coil units from 250 to 1,250 CFM offered in exposed flat-top, slope-top, and concealed wall-recessed cabinets for commercial and institutional perimeters.

Key points:
- 250 to 1,250 CFM vertical perimeter capacities match glass curtain walls and classroom exteriors
- Exposed architectural heavy-gauge steel cabinets (flat-top or slope-top) or concealed wall-recessed housings
- Configured with 2-pipe or 4-pipe hydronic coils, optional electric heat, and factory valve piping packages
- AHRI 440 certified and UL 1995 listed for reliable, serviceable zone conditioning
