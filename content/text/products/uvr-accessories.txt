Name: UV-C Accessories

Summary: Safety and monitoring accessories for UV-C installations, including lamp hour meters, UV-C radiometers, door-interlock switches, viewport glass, and BMS alarm relays. Helps facilities teams verify UV-C output and protect technicians during AHU service.

Key points:
- Lamp runtime hour meters and calibrated UV-C radiometers
- Door-interlock kill switches and UV-blocking viewport glass
- 4–20mA monitors and current-sensing relays for BMS lamp-out alarms
