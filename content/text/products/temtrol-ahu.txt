Name: Custom Air Handlers

Summary: Temtrol custom air handlers span 1,000 to 150,000+ CFM using 3" or 3.5" double-wall Integrated Frame (ITF) construction on welded structural tube base frames with injected foam (R-24) or fiberglass (R-12.5). Rated to <1% leakage and L/200 to L/240 up to ±10" WC with no thru-metal except fasteners, stacked air tunnels, slide-out coil racks, third-layer standing seam roofs to 39'-8", and Miami-Dade / OSHPD certifications.

Key points:
- 3" or 3.5" ITF frame on welded structural tube base (R-24 injected foam or R-12.5 fiberglass; no thru-metal except fasteners)
- Less than 1% casing leakage at ±8" WC and L/240 deflection up to ±10" WC with perforated acoustic liners
- Slide-out coil racks with intermediate drain pans, third-layer standing seam roof to 39'-8", and Rapid Knockdown for tight ingress
- Miami-Dade Intertek TAS-201/202/203 and OSHPD seismic certifications, BABA options, and Gen IV FANWALL arrays
