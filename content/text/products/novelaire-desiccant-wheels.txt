Name: Desiccant Dehumidification Wheels

Summary: Manufactured in Baton Rouge, LA across 300 to 50,000+ CFM sizes, NovelAire desiccant wheels cover three media chemistries: 4A molecular sieve (ECW passive enthalpy), activated alumina (DES low-temp 90–150°F waste/condenser heat regen), and silica gel (FSG / LT3 / WSG washable matrices for 120–350°F high-capacity drying). Slow 8–24 RPH wheel rotation supports 50/50 or 75/25 airstream splits.

Key points:
- Three specialized media options: 4A molecular sieve (ECW), activated alumina (DES), and silica gel (FSG / LT3 / WSG washable)
- Activated alumina (DES) regenerates at low 90–150°F waste/hot-water/condenser heat; LT3 silica gel uses 120–180°F and WSG/FSG uses 150–350°F (<300°F typ.)
- Engineered cassette rotations of 8–24 RPH with 50/50 (DES) or 75/25 (DH) process-to-regen splits on rigid fiberglass matrices
- Custom OEM and drop-in replacement wheels from 300 to 50,000+ CFM backed by SelectDRY performance modeling

Specifications:
- Media chemistries: 4A molecular sieve (ECW passive), activated alumina (DES low-temp), and silica gel (FSG / LT3 / WSG washable) on fiberglass matrix
- Regeneration temps: 90–150°F on activated alumina (DES); 120–180°F on LT3 silica gel; 150–350°F (typ. <300°F) on FSG/WSG silica gel
- Rotation & splits: 8–24 rotations/hr speed-controlled drive; 50/50 (DES) or 75/25 (DH) airstream split
- Airflow & sizing: 300 to 50,000+ CFM OEM and replacement cassettes modeled in SelectDRY software
