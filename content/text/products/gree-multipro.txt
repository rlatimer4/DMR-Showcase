Name: MultiPRO R32 Ultra Heat Pump

Summary: MultiPRO R-32 Ultra multi-zone heat pump systems (18,000 to 48,000 BTU/h) feature a two-stage EVI compressor delivering cold-climate heating down to −22°F (−30°C). Extended line-set lengths link cassette, ducted, wall-mounted, and air handler indoor units via branch boxes or multi-port headers.

Key points:
- 18,000 to 48,000 BTU/h cold-climate R-32 multi-zone heat pump system
- Two-stage EVI inverter compressor maintains heating down to −22°F (−30°C)
- Extended line sets pair cassette, ducted, wall-mount, and air handler indoor units
