Name: VH Vertical Hi-Rise Fan Coil Units

Summary: Vertical hi-rise fan coil units delivering 350 to 1,200 CFM per unit that stack floor-to-floor on factory-insulated risers to trim field piping labor in hotels, apartments, and dormitories.

Key points:
- 350 to 1,200 CFM vertical stack units ship with factory-insulated 3/4" to 3" copper risers for fast rough-in
- Single or master/secondary twin configurations serve adjacent guestrooms or multifamily suites from one chase
- Multiple 2-pipe and 4-pipe coil row/circuiting options with factory valve piping packages
- AHRI 440 certified and UL 1995 listed for multi-story residential and hospitality construction
