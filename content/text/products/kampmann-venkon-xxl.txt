Name: Venkon XXL High-Static Blower Coils

Summary: Venkon XXL is the ducted blower coil member of the Kampmann Venkon family. Four sizes of ducted recirculation units handle extra-high external static pressures up to 4" H2O, for long duct runs and multi-diffuser zones that a fan coil cannot reach.

Key points:
- Ducted blower coil in 4 sizes for long duct runs and multi-diffuser zones
- Up to 4" H2O external static pressure
- EC fan with 2-pipe or 4-pipe hydronic coils, from the VDI 6022 hygienic Venkon platform
