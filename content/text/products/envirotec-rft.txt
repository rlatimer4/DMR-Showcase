Name: RFT-MS Retrofit Measuring Station

Summary: RFT-MS retrofit airflow measuring stations slide into round 4" to 16" or rectangular ducts to restore accurate airflow control without demolishing existing ductwork. Combines a multi-point averaging airflow cross, damper blade, and DDC or pneumatic actuator to upgrade constant-volume or failed VAV boxes.

Key points:
- Fits round 4" to 16" and rectangular ducts without removing existing ductwork
- Includes multi-point averaging airflow cross, damper blade, and DDC/pneumatic actuator
- Converts constant-volume or failed VAV zones to modern DDC control
