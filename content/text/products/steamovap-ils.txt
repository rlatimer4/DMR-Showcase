Name: ILS Live Steam Humidifier

Summary: Pressurized live-steam injection humidifiers delivering 4 to 3,300 lb/h (2 to 1,500 kg/h) by separating condensate and injecting dry boiler steam evenly across AHUs and ductwork.

Key points:
- High capacity range from 4 to 3,300 lb/h (2 to 1,500 kg/h) driven directly by central plant steam at 2 to 50 psig
- Stainless steel centrifugal steam separator and heated jacket ensure condensate-free, drip-free dispersion
- Paired with electric or pneumatic modulating control valves for tight duct relative humidity control
- Ideal for large institutional campuses, hospitals, and universities with existing steam distribution mains
