Name: Diphusair FSH Humidifiers

Summary: Diphusair FSH humidifiers inject 5 to 1,800 kg/h (11 to 3,960 lb/h) of dry clean steam from 0.2 to 4.0 bar (3 to 58 psig) pressurized boiler steam supplies. Built with 316L TIG-welded stainless steel jacketed drying chambers, dispersion lances, and modulating pneumatic or electric control valves.

Key points:
- 5 to 1,800 kg/h (11 to 3,960 lb/h) capacity from 0.2–4.0 bar (3–58 psig) boiler steam
- 316L TIG-welded stainless steel jacketed drying chamber and dispersion lance
- Integrated steam separator ensures condensate-free dry steam discharge
- Pneumatic or electric modulating control valve (0–10V / 4–20mA)
