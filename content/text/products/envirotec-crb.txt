Name: CRB Series Flow Fan Powered VAV Terminals

Summary: CRB / CR series-flow fan-powered VAV terminal units span 100 to 4,400 CFM delivering constant, low-sound airflow while blending primary air with warm plenum return air. Equipped with brushless ECM motors, easy-service CR cabinet access, and the FlowStar™ inlet sensor yielding 230% to 290% signal amplification down to 20% airflow turndown.

Key points:
- Series-flow ECM fan delivers constant zone discharge volume across 100 to 4,400 CFM
- FlowStar™ primary inlet sensor provides 230–290% amplification (0.04" w.g. @ 480 FPM / 20% flow) for tight low-end control
- New CR series cabinet refinements improve serviceability and whisper-quiet operation in offices and conference rooms
- AHRI 880 certified and UL 1995 listed with 1- to 3-row hot water coils or staged/SCR electric reheat
