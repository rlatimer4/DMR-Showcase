Name: GLO Upper-Air UV-C Fixtures

Summary: GLO upper-air germicidal UV-C fixtures treat 200 to 500 sq ft per wall-mounted fixture to disinfect room air above occupants' heads in healthcare clinics, schools, and shelters. Precision parabolic baffle louvers confine the 254 nm beam strictly above the 7 ft occupied zone per CDC, NIOSH, and ASHRAE guidance.
