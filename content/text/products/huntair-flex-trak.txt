Name: CleanPak Flex-Trak T Grid Ceiling System

Summary: CleanPak Flex-Trak extruded aluminum cleanroom T-grid ceilings (1.5" and 2" profiles) provide fast modular framing for pharmaceutical, biotech, medical-device, and microelectronics cleanrooms. Engineered to support suspended CleanPak fan filter units, walkable panels, blank tiles, and cleanroom luminaires.

Key points:
- 1.5" and 2" extruded aluminum cleanroom T-grid profiles in standard module sizes
- Engineered for pharmaceutical, biotech, medical-device, and electronics cleanrooms
- Loads accommodate walkable panels, CleanPak FFUs, and cleanroom luminaires
