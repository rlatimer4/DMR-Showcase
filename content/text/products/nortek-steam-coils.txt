Name: Standard Steam Coils

Summary: Standard Nortek steam heating coils use heavy-wall 5/8" and 1" OD copper or cupronickel tubes mechanically expanded into aluminum or copper fins inside pitched casings. Engineered for 2 to 200 psig saturated steam service with positive condensate drainage and AHRI 410 certification.

Key points:
- Heavy-wall 5/8" and 1" OD seamless copper or cupronickel tubes
- Rated for 2 to 200 psig saturated steam with pitched casing for condensate drainage
- AHRI 410 certified capacity in custom dimensions for new and replacement coils
