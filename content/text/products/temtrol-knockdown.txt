Name: Knockdown Air Handlers

Summary: Temtrol Rapid Knockdown air handlers use 3" or 3.5" double-wall ITF framed construction on welded structural tube base frames, factory-assembled with minimal fasteners and caulk for straightforward field disassembly during tight ingress. Rated to ±10" WC at L/240 deflection with foam or fiberglass insulation, no thru-metal except fasteners, and optional perforated liners.

Key points:
- Rapid Knockdown built with minimal fasteners and caulk for field disassembly at ingress
- 3" or 3.5" double-wall ITF frame on welded structural tube base (±10" WC, L/240 deflection)
- No thru-metal except fasteners; injected/spray polyurethane foam or fiberglass + perforated liners
- Stacked air tunnels, Seismic and Miami-Dade certifications, and BABA compliance options
