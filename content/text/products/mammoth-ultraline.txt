Name: UltraLine Custom Packaged Rooftop Systems

Summary: Mammoth UltraLine custom packaged rooftop systems now span 5 to 380 tons and 1,000 to 300,000+ CFM in 3" double-wall ITF framed construction on welded tube base frames (±10" WC @ L/240). Redesigned Vee ACCU microchannel kits (31" MaEr EC fans, 105°F standard ambient), VFD compressors (up to 40 tons/circuit), R-454B, −20°F Liquid Injection ASHP options, and NASDA-integrated electrical FLA/MCA/MOP calculations lower weight and cost.

Key points:
- Expanded custom DX capacity from 5 to 380 tons (1,000–300,000+ CFM) with 3" ITF double-wall casing (±10" WC @ L/240)
- Standardized Vee ACCU microchannel kits (105°F design ambient, 31" MaEr EC condenser fans; 80", 94", and 136" min widths)
- VFD compressors across all condenser types (up to 40 tons/circuit), R-454B, and −20°F Liquid Injection ASHP capability
- 2" recessed Polyurea washdown floor option, curb-match footprints, ER wheel/plate, and NASDA-calculated FLA/MCA/MOP electrical data
