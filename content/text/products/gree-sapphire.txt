Name: Sapphire R32 Mini-Split

Summary: Sapphire R-32 is GREE's flagship high-efficiency single-zone mini-split (9,000 to 24,000 BTU/h) achieving up to 28.0 SEER2 and 11.5 HSPF2 under ENERGY STAR Most Efficient criteria. Powered by a two-stage EVI compressor for continuous cold-climate heating down to −22°F (−30°C).

Key points:
- Flagship 9,000 to 24,000 BTU/h single-zone mini-split with R-32 refrigerant
- Up to 28.0 SEER2 and 11.5 HSPF2 (ENERGY STAR Most Efficient rated)
- Two-stage EVI compressor delivers continuous heating down to −22°F (−30°C)
