Name: Convectors

Summary: Sterling natural-convection hydronic convectors provide 2,000 to 45,000 BTU/h using high-efficiency copper/aluminum heating cores on hot water or two-pipe steam systems. Configured in free-standing, semi-recessed, fully recessed, wall-hung, and sloping-top architectural cabinets.
