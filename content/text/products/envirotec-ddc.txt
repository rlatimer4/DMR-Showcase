Name: Configurable VAV DDC Controls

Summary: Factory-mounted and wired direct digital controls pre-configured and airflow-calibrated inside a NEMA enclosure for Enviro-Tec VAV terminals. Supports native BACnet MS/TP, BACnet IP, or LonWorks protocols with integral differential pressure transducers and sensor ports.

Key points:
- Factory-mounted, wired, and airflow-calibrated inside a protective NEMA enclosure
- Native BACnet MS/TP, BACnet IP, or LonWorks field-configurable controllers
- Integral differential pressure transducer with supply air and room sensor ports
