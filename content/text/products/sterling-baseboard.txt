Name: Kompak and Senior Baseboard

Summary: Kompak (7.5" high) and Senior (9.4" high) hydronic baseboards pair a 3/4" copper tube and aluminum fins on full-length acoustic expansion cradles for silent heating. Delivers 550 to 940 BTU/h per linear foot at 180°F EWT with a built-in fingertip damper for local room control.

Key points:
- Kompak (7.5" height) and Senior (9.4" height) profiles deliver 550–940 BTU/h per ft
- 3/4" copper tube with aluminum fins on full-length acoustic expansion cradles
- Fingertip damper provides local room comfort trim with silent operation
