Name: EnergyPack Custom HVAC Solution

Summary: Venmar CES EnergyPack systems combine custom energy recovery with heating, cooling, and packaged refrigeration in a 3" double-wall ITF framed casing on a welded structural tube base. Rated to ±10" WC at L/240 deflection with polyurethane spray foam or fiberglass insulation, no thru-metal except fasteners, stacked air tunnels, perforated liners, and a 2" recessed Polyurea washdown floor option.

Key points:
- 3" double-wall ITF frame on welded structural tube base (±10" WC, L/240 deflection)
- No thru-metal except fasteners; polyurethane spray foam or fiberglass + perforated liners
- 2" recessed floor with watertight insulating Polyurea membrane & washdown option
- AHRI 1060 wheels, plates, heat pipes, or membranes + packaged refrigeration & stacked tunnels
