Name: Cabinet Unit Heaters

Summary: Sterling cabinet unit heaters deliver 200 to 1,800 CFM and 10,000 to 135,000 BTU/h of forced-air hot water or steam heat for entrances, vestibules, stairwells, and corridors. Offered in floor, wall, ceiling, and recessed mounts across 8 ducted or free-blow styles with PSC or ECM motors.
