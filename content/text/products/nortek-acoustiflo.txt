Name: Air Handlers with AcoustiFLO Fans

Summary: Custom air handlers from the Nortek Air Solutions brands (Temtrol, Governair, Huntair, Mammoth, Venmar, and Ventrol) fitted with AcoustiFLO mixed-flow fans for noise-critical venues. Pairs 10–20 dB lower 250–500 Hz sound power with welded structural tube bases, polyurethane spray/injected foam casings, and optional perforated acoustic liners (all except Temtrol LC & Huntair FPC).

Key points:
- 10 to 20 dB quieter at 250–500 Hz; >10% higher AMCA peak static efficiency than plenum arrays
- Optimized for 5,000 to 25,000 CFM per fan module, often eliminating sound attenuators
- Built on welded structural tube bases with brand-specific ITF, smooth panelized, or FPC casings
- Perforated acoustic liners available (all except Temtrol LC & Huntair FPC) + stacked air tunnels
