Name: Air Handlers with AcoustiFLO Fans

Summary: Custom air handlers from the Nortek Air Solutions brands (Temtrol, Governair, Huntair, Mammoth, Venmar and Ventrol) fitted with AcoustiFLO fans, for projects where low noise matters, such as performing arts halls.

Key points:
- 10 to 20 dB quieter in the 250 to 500 Hz bands
- Often removes the need for sound attenuators
- Optimized for 5,000 to 25,000 CFM
- Over 10% higher peak static efficiency than plenum fan arrays (AMCA certified)
