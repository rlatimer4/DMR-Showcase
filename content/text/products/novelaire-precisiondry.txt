Name: PrecisionDRY Packaged Dehumidification Units

Summary: NovelAire PrecisionDRY (DH series) active desiccant dehumidifiers span 1,000 to 15,000 CFM standard (up to 30,000 CFM custom)—plus the new PrecisionDRY Apex (150–1,000 CFM)—to reach −5°F to +38°F supply dewpoints (custom down to −40°F DP). High-temperature 150–285°F reactivation (up to 350°F silica gel limit) via gas, electric, or steam pairs with mineral/rock wool insulation and a 75/25 process-to-regen wheel split.

Key points:
- DH series (1,000–15,000 CFM std; custom to 30,000 CFM) plus PrecisionDRY Apex (150–1,000 CFM) reach −5°F to +38°F DP (to −40°F DP custom)
- High-temperature 150–285°F (max 350°F) reactivation via direct/indirect gas, electric, or steam with mineral/rock wool high-temp insulation
- 75/25 process-to-regen wheel split maximizes moisture removal in a compact footprint (proven at Cloverdale DH15000-ERV & Archie Browning DH8000)
- Optional ERV section, DX/CW pre- & post-cooling, heat recovery coil on regen exhaust, ECM/VFD fans, and BACnet BMS or standalone HMI
