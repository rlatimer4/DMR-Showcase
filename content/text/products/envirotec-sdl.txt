Name: SDL Single Duct Low-Height VAV Terminals

Summary: SDL single-duct low-height VAV terminal units deliver 100 to 2,800 CFM inside compact 8", 10", and 11" cabinet heights for constrained plenums. Equipped with low-static-drop multi-point averaging flow sensors, hot water or staged/SSR electric reheat, and AHRI 880 certification.

Key points:
- Compact 8", 10", and 11" casing heights handle 100 to 2,800 CFM primary air
- Multi-point averaging flow sensor minimizes inlet static pressure drop
- Optional integral hot water coil or staged/SSR electric duct reheat
- AHRI 880 certified performance and UL 1995 listed
