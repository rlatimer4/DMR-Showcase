Name: Direct Expansion Evaporator Coils

Summary: Custom Nortek direct expansion evaporator coils use 3/8", 1/2", and 5/8" OD seamless copper tubes mechanically expanded into fins and pressure-tested above 450 psig. Configurable with single, dual, intertwined, or face-split circuiting for R-410A, R-32, R-454B, R-134a, and R-513A under AHRI 410.

Key points:
- 3/8", 1/2", and 5/8" OD rifled or smooth seamless copper tubes expanded into fins
- Single, dual, intertwined, or face-split circuits with brass distributor nozzles
- Compatible with R-410A, R-32, R-454B, R-134a, and R-513A refrigerants
- AHRI 410 certified performance and UL 1995 pressure-tested at 450+ psig
