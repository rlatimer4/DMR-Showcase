Name: SLN Xtreme UV-C Fixtures

Summary: A single-lamp very high output NEMA-4 UV-C fixture built for moist airstreams, cooling coils, and drain pans in rooftop units and ducts. Its moisture-sealed enclosure and 16" to 61" lamps maintain coil heat-transfer efficiency in harsh HVAC environments.

Key points:
- Moisture-resistant NEMA-4 enclosure
- Engineered for rooftop units, cooling coils, and drain pans
- 16" to 61" very high output 254 nm germicidal lamps
- Universal 120V–277V electronic ballast, UL 1995 listed
