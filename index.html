Why DMR Associates

Engineering support that lives every project from concept to commissioning.

Serving Washington, D.C., Maryland and Northern Virginia since 1969, DMR Associates pairs premier manufacturers with hands-on local application engineering.

Single-Point Design-Assist

Every DMR salesperson lives your project from early schematic design and psychrometric/acoustic sizing through submittals, factory witness testing, start-up and closeout.

Occupied Retrofit Expertise

Decades of field experience modernizing occupied DMV office towers, hospitals and laboratories — engineering knockdown modules, split-width cabinets and curb-match packages to slash downtime.

Coordinated Multi-Line Packages

Coordinate air handling, FANWALL® redundancy, energy recovery, coils, desiccant dehumidification, steam humidification, germicidal UV-C and VRF/fan-coil zones through one accountable team.

Proven in the DMV

Published DMR retrofit case studies.

Real projects featured by DMR Associates where custom engineering solved access, footprint and schedule constraints in occupied regional buildings.

Arlington, VA · High-Rise Office

Arlington Tower (NEMA Headquarters)

Upgraded the HVAC system across a 19-story, 398,000+ sq. ft. occupied office tower delivering 294,000 CFM and 980 nominal tons via sectionalized Mammoth V-Cube Slim units engineered to roll through standard 36-inch doors and service elevators with zero field refrigerant breaks.

Chevy Chase, MD · Luxury Condominiums

Parc Somerset High-Rise Retrofit

Engineered custom replacement Mammoth UltraLine 100% outside air units tailored to extremely tight mechanical clearances while aligning precisely with existing structural connections, ductwork transitions and electrical infrastructure.

Chantilly, VA · Commercial Office

Corporate Oaks II

Executed an occupied commercial office modernization built to minimize tenant disruption by reusing the existing roof curb, ductwork and utility connections while upgrading legacy aluminum electrical feeders to copper.
